# **Case Study: Revenue Recovery from Untapped Applicants**
### *Regional Home Services Company (MA/CT)*

---

## **The Situation**
A regional home services company was generating strong applicant demand through Indeed.

However:
- ~20,000 applicants were sitting unprocessed
- The hiring process was entirely manual
- No structured pipeline or tracking system existed

To process a single applicant, the workflow required:
- 5+ manual text messages to collect basic information
- Delays between each response
- Manual scheduling of a Zoom call
- Only then, potential hiring

**Result:** The system became too slow and cumbersome to scale.

At one point, Indeed intake was turned off entirely.

---

## **The Real Problem**
This was not a hiring issue.

It was a **conversion failure between applicants and revenue generation.**

Each unprocessed applicant represented:
- A potential recruiter
- A future in-home demo
- A future sale

The backlog wasn’t just data — it was **unrealized revenue.**

---

## **What Was Breaking**
- Excessive manual back-and-forth (5+ texts per applicant)
- Long delays between touchpoints
- High drop-off before scheduling
- No qualification system
- No prioritization of strong candidates
- No visibility into pipeline status

**System Effect:**

Applicants → Manual texting → Delays → Drop-off → Bottleneck → Hiring stalls → Revenue caps

---

## **Solution: Recruiter Throughput System**
A structured system was designed to eliminate friction and convert applicants into active revenue capacity.

### **New System Flow**
Applicants → Data Processing → Qualification → Routing → Outreach → Scheduling → Activation

### **Core Components**
- Raw data intake (no data corruption)
- Data normalization and deduplication
- AI-assisted qualification
- Clear pipeline stages (Qualified / Review / Reject)
- Follow-up tracking (last contact / next action)
- Streamlined outreach and scheduling flow

---

## **What Changed**
**Before:**
- 5+ manual messages per applicant
- Slow, inconsistent follow-up
- High candidate drop-off
- No scalability

**After:**
- Applicants processed in batches
- Qualified candidates surfaced instantly
- Faster path to scheduling
- Reduced reliance on manual back-and-forth
- System capable of handling high volume

---

## **Business Impact**
- Eliminated missed opportunities from backlog
- Reduced time-to-contact significantly
- Increased recruiter activation speed
- Enabled hiring to scale with demand
- Transformed recruiting into a revenue-driving function

---

## **Key Insight**
> The problem wasn’t too many applicants.
> 
> It was too much friction between interest and activation.

---

## **Positioning Statement**
> The company didn’t stop hiring because of too many applicants —
> 
> they stopped because their system couldn’t convert them into revenue.
