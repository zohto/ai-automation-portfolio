# AI-Powered Customer Support Workflow

A workflow design study (TripleTen AI Automation, Project 1) proposing three AI integrations for a multi-channel customer support process: chatbot intake and initial triage, NLP-based ticket classification and smart routing, and AI-assisted investigation with knowledge surfacing.

The deck documents the current-state workflow, the key problems identified (long wait times, misrouted tickets, slow investigation), the proposed AI-enhanced workflow, and a phased rollout plan with monitoring and training requirements.

**Approach:** Process audit · AI integration design · before/after workflow modeling
